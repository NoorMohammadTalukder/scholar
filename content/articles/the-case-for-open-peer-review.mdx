## The last closed door

Data are open. Code is open. Preprints are open. The reviews that decide what becomes an article remain hidden, and with them the reasoning that readers most need.

## What the evidence says

Trials at several journals found that signed, published reviews were longer, more constructive, and no less critical. Reviewer decline rates rose modestly.

## Our policy

From Volume 2, we publish reviews and author responses for every article where all parties consent. Reviewers may choose to sign or remain anonymous. We will report participation rates annually.
