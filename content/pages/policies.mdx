---
## Peer review

Every submission is assessed by the handling editor and then sent to at least two referees from different disciplines. We use open peer review: reviews and author responses are published with the article when all parties consent. Referees may sign their reviews or remain anonymous. Median time to first decision is 38 days.

## Open access and fees

All articles are published under a [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence. There are no submission or publication charges. The journal is funded by its publisher and institutional supporters.

## Data and code

Authors must make the data and code needed to reproduce their results available in a public repository with a persistent identifier, unless legal or ethical constraints prevent it. Exceptions must be explained in the manuscript.

## Research ethics

Studies involving human participants or animals must report ethical approval. We follow the guidelines of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) for handling misconduct, corrections, and retractions.

## Preprints

We encourage posting preprints and will consider manuscripts that have been posted to any recognised preprint server.

## Archiving

Articles are deposited with Crossref, archived in Portico, and indexed in DOAJ.
