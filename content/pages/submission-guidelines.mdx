## Before you submit

Read our [aims and scope](/about). Ask yourself whether a referee from a second discipline is necessary to judge your work. If not, we are probably not the right venue.

## Manuscript preparation

- Submit a single PDF for review. Source files (LaTeX or Word) are requested on acceptance.
- Use our [manuscript template](/pdfs/scholar-template.pdf) or any clean single-column format.
- Include a structured abstract of up to 250 words and 4–6 keywords.
- Define every discipline-specific term on first use.
- Number figures and tables consecutively and place captions below figures and above tables.
- Use any consistent citation style; we will convert accepted manuscripts to house style.

## Data and code availability

Include a statement listing where data and code can be obtained. Use a repository that issues DOIs (Zenodo, OSF, Dryad, or a discipline-specific archive).

## Author contributions

Use the CRediT taxonomy to describe each author's contribution.

## How to submit

Email your PDF and a cover letter to the editorial office. The cover letter should name the two or more disciplines the work draws on and suggest up to four potential referees, at least one from each discipline.
