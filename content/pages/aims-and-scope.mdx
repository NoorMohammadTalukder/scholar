---
title: Aims and scope
---
*Scholar: Journal of Interdisciplinary Research* publishes original research, reviews, and perspectives that draw on two or more disciplines and, in doing so, ask questions neither could ask alone.

We are deliberately broad in subject matter and deliberately narrow in what we value: methodological rigour, clarity for readers outside the authors' home fields, and openness of data and code.

## What we publish

- **Research articles** reporting original empirical or theoretical work (typically 6,000–10,000 words).
- **Reviews** that synthesise a cross-disciplinary literature for a general scholarly reader.
- **Short communications** of up to 3,000 words reporting a single significant result.
- **Perspectives** arguing a position on research practice, policy, or method.

## What we do not publish

Work that could appear unchanged in a single-discipline venue. If your paper's contribution can be fully judged by referees from one field, it belongs in that field's journal.
