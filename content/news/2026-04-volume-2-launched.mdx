Volume 2, Issue 1 collects three articles on learning in physical and economic systems. It also marks the start of open peer review at *Scholar*: for every article where authors and referees consent, we now publish the reviews and the authors' responses.

Read the [Editor-in-Chief's perspective](/articles/the-case-for-open-peer-review) on why we made this change.
