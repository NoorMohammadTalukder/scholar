Every field builds models, and every field has learned, sometimes painfully, where they break. For our Autumn 2026 issue we invite work that takes those limits seriously.

We welcome research articles, reviews, and perspectives on topics including but not limited to:

- Model validation and the epistemology of simulation
- Failures of extrapolation in climate, epidemiological, and economic models
- Benchmarks, leaderboards, and the measurement of machine capability
- Historical and philosophical studies of modelling practice

Submissions are due **30 November 2026**. See our [submission guidelines](/for-authors) for formatting and review details.
