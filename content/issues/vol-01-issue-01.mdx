Welcome to the founding issue of *Scholar*. When we began planning this journal, a recurring frustration came up in nearly every conversation: the most interesting work our colleagues were doing had no obvious home. A paper that combined Bayesian ecology with field ethnography was "too methods-heavy" for one venue and "not ecological enough" for another.

This issue is our answer. The three articles collected here share nothing in subject matter and everything in spirit. Each borrows a method from a neighbouring discipline and, in doing so, asks a question the home discipline could not have posed alone.

We are grateful to our founding editorial board, to the reviewers who gave their time to an unproven venue, and to the authors who trusted us with their work.
