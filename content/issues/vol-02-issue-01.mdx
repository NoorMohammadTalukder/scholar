Our second volume opens with a question that has fascinated engineers and economists alike: how do systems learn from limited experience? Henrik Lindqvist's team shows how a quadruped can learn to walk on rubble after training only in simulation. Samuel Mensah traces how households in Ghana learned to use mobile money, and what it did for their welfare.

We close with a perspective from our Editor-in-Chief arguing that peer review should itself be open to scrutiny, an argument that will shape this journal's policies going forward.
